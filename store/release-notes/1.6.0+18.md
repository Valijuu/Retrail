## en
New:
• History map shows your location right away; tap the dot to draw the circle around you
• Ride menu: "Show on map"

• The ride timer no longer runs slow in the background
• Ending a ride from the lock screen no longer starts a new one on opening
• Rides at the same place show as a dot on the map
• Profile photo survives the update on iPhone
• Error messages instead of endless loading
Android: rides and profile photo are no longer in the Google backup.
